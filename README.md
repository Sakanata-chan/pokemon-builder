# ⚔️ Pokémon Team Builder (Gen 1 – 5)

A responsive, browser-based Pokémon Team Builder application designed for competitive strategy and team planning across Generations 1 through 5 (Kanto to Unova). Powered by the official [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co/), this tool allows trainers to assemble a 6-Pokémon roster, customize individual stats/movesets, analyze team defensive coverage in real-time, and export configurations directly into Competitive Showdown format.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Complete Gen 1–5 Pokédex (649 Species):** Fast full-text search and generation filtering with dynamic auto-suggestions.
* **Full Stat & EV/IV Customizer:**
* Adjust level (1–100), gender, shiny variants, and back/front sprite toggles.
* Real-time statutory calculations accounting for Natures, IVs (0–31), and EVs (0–252).
* One-click competitive EV/IV presets (*Physical Sweeper*, *Special Sweeper*, *Bulky Pivot*, *Trick Room*, etc.).


* **Dynamic Moveset & Ability Info:** Detailed move tooltips including Power, Accuracy, PP, Category (Physical/Special/Status), and effect descriptions.
* **Real-Time Defensive Matrix:** Live calculation of your team's collective weaknesses, resistances, and type immunities across all 17 Pokémon types.
* **Batch Editing:** Apply uniform changes (Levels, Natures, Friendship levels, Shininess) across all 6 team members simultaneously.
* **Pokémon Showdown Export:** Instantly convert your custom team into standard Showdown text format ready for copying.
* **Interactive UI & Themes:** Dark/Light mode toggle, animated sprite interactions, and authentic Pokémon sound effects (cries).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML5, Modern CSS3 (CSS Variables, Flexbox, Grid), JavaScript (ES6+)
* **API:** [PokéAPI v2](https://pokeapi.co/) (REST API)
* **Assets:** GitHub Raw Sprites, Official Pokémon Cries
